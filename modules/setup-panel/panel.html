Cert pools

Second label on each ops line. Function stays DFO / BAG / PAX. Function map wins — every DFO is B even if that hour goes over the target. 45% B is a best-effort target for every operational hour of every day inside the slice. A person counts only on days they work, and only in the hours their shift covers. Part-time counts for those hours, not as a full open-to-close body. After forced B labels, remaining B labels go to people who cover hours that are still short. Anyone still unlabeled becomes A. Slices stay STSO, LTSO, and TSO × sex (TSO men = FT+PT; same for women). Ops Yes extras use their own type name × sex slice and are not folded into TSO. Ops No extras stay blank and out of every slice.
